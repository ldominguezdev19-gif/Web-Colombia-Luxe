Selected curatorship

The most coveted destinations

Discover Colombia through an ultra-exclusive lens of distinction, private, comfort and restricted priviliges crafted for our most discerning travelers.

Cartagena de Indias

A walled colonial city on the Caribbean, all cobblestone streets and private rooftop sunsets.

Medellín

The city of eternal spring — skyline penthouses, refined dining, and a nightlife that never quite ends.

Eje Cafetero

Misty mountain haciendas among giant wax palms, where the world's finest coffee is grown and served.

Santa Marta

Home to Tayrona National Park and the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta, the highest coastal mountain range on Earth.

Barranquilla

Caribbean sophistication with a business edge — riverfront glamour and the pulse of Carnival.
